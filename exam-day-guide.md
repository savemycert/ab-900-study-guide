# AB-900 Exam-Day Guide

This page covers the logistics of the Microsoft 365 Certified: Copilot and Agent Administration Fundamentals (AB-900) exam: booking, delivery options, check-in, exam format, scoring, and what happens afterward. Every fact links to the official Microsoft Learn page it came from. Policies change, so confirm anything time-sensitive on the linked page before your appointment.

> Also see the [AB-900 practice questions](https://www.savemycert.com/practice/copilot-and-agent-administration-fundamentals/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=ab-900-study-guide) and [timed mock exams](https://www.savemycert.com/mocks/copilot-and-agent-administration-fundamentals/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=ab-900-study-guide).

## Booking the exam

On the certification page, choose **Schedule exam** under Pearson VUE. Students and educators can schedule through Certiport instead. You may be asked to sign in to your Learn profile and add a legal name, which must match your ID or you won't be able to take the exam. You then add an address, phone number and contact email before Pearson VUE takes over. Microsoft strongly recommends a personal Microsoft account (MSA): exam records tied to a work or school account are lost if you leave that organization, and they can't be recovered.

You can book no more than 90 days ahead, and you can hold at most two Microsoft Certification exams at a time through Pearson VUE. Reschedule or cancel from your Learn profile at least 24 hours before the appointment, or your fee is forfeited.

The price depends on the country or region where the exam is proctored, so check the certification page for yours. The exam is offered in 13 languages: English, Arabic (Saudi Arabia), Japanese, Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), Korean, German, French, Indonesian (Indonesia), Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian and Spanish.

Source: [Copilot and Agent Administration Fundamentals certification](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/copilot-and-agent-administration-fundamentals/), [Register and schedule an exam](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/register-schedule-exam), [Reschedule and cancellation policies](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/support/reschedule-and-cancellation-policy)

## Online (OnVUE) vs test center

The exam is proctored. In most cases you can take it online or at a Pearson VUE test center. A test center gives you a quiet, pre-configured room with no software to install. Online, you are responsible for your computer and room meeting the security rules:

- Run Pearson VUE's system test on the same computer, connection and location before you register, and again on exam day. A personal computer is safer than a work one, since work machines often block the OnVUE software.
- Use a walled room with a closed door. No one else may be in or enter the room during the exam.
- Clear the work area of books, notepads, notes and pens, unplug and turn away extra monitors, and turn off other computers.
- Keep your phone out of arm's reach but close enough to hear, in case the proctor calls. A drink is allowed in a transparent, spill-proof container.
- Greeters and proctors communicate in English (with limited Japanese availability), even if your exam is in another language.

Source: [Register and schedule an exam](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/register-schedule-exam), [About online exams with Pearson VUE](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/online-exams)

## ID and check-in

You need a current government-issued ID, and the name on it must exactly match the name on your Microsoft Certification profile. Pearson VUE's ID policy lists acceptable documents. For online exams, legal name-change and ID renewal forms are not accepted.

For an online exam, check in from your Learn profile between 30 minutes before and 15 minutes after your appointment time. You capture your headshot, ID and exam area with your phone, and a greeter reviews them before placing you in the proctor queue. Launch and check-in take about 15 minutes.

Source: [About online exams with Pearson VUE](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/online-exams)

## How the exam is structured

You have 45 minutes to complete the exam. For Fundamentals exams, Microsoft suggests allowing 65 minutes of seat time for instructions, the candidate agreement and optional comments. Most Microsoft exams have 40 to 60 questions, but the number varies by exam. The certification page notes that you may have interactive components to complete.

Microsoft does not name the question types in advance. The free exam sandbox lets you try the types it may use, such as multiple choice, drag and drop, build list, hot area, active screen and case studies. Fundamentals exams do not give you access to Microsoft Learn during the test. Microsoft also offers a free practice assessment on the certification page.

Five minutes are built in for unscheduled breaks. The exam clock keeps running during a break, and afterward you cannot return to any question you saw before it.

The skills measured, as of October 14, 2026, are: Identify the core features and objects of Microsoft 365 services (30–35%), Understand data protection and governance tasks for Microsoft 365 and Copilot (35–40%), and Perform basic administrative tasks for Copilot and agents (25–30%). Most questions cover generally available features, but commonly used preview features can appear.

Source: [Copilot and Agent Administration Fundamentals certification](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/copilot-and-agent-administration-fundamentals/), [Exam duration and exam experience](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/support/exam-duration-exam-experience), [AB-900 study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/ab-900)

## How scoring works

Scores are reported on a scale of 1 to 1,000, and you need 700 or more to pass. Because the score is scaled, 700 does not necessarily mean 70% of the points.

On most multi-part questions you earn one point per correct part, so partial credit is possible. There is no penalty for guessing. Some questions are unscored, and you can't tell which ones, so answer every question.

Source: [Exam scoring and score reports](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exam-scoring-reports)

## Results and score report

For most exams you see your result within minutes of finishing. The score report shows your overall score, pass or fail, and a bar chart of your performance in each skill area. The bars show strengths and weaknesses, but you can't use them to work out how many questions you got right. Microsoft does not tell you which questions you missed. Your score appears in your Learn profile within 24 hours.

Source: [Exam scoring and score reports](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exam-scoring-reports)

## Your credential

When you pass, the certification appears in your Learn profile under Credentials with an Online Verifiable tag. From there you can share it, for example on LinkedIn, and download and print a certificate. Others can verify a shared credential only while your Learn profile is public.

Fundamentals certifications do not expire, so there is no renewal for AB-900.

Source: [Sharing and validating of Microsoft Credentials](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/cred-share-validate), [Microsoft Certification Renewal](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/renew-your-microsoft-certification)

## If you don't pass

- After a first failed attempt, wait 24 hours before retaking.
- After that, wait 14 days between attempts.
- You can take the same exam at most five times in 12 months, counted from your first attempt.
- You pay for each retake (if applicable).
- A waiver of the waiting period is granted only for internet connectivity issues or equipment failure with a Pearson VUE case number.

Source: [Exam retake policy](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/support/retake-policy)

## On exam day: practical tips

- **Try the sandbox first.** It uses the real exam's interface, navigation and review screen.
- **Watch the clock.** 45 minutes is short. Mark hard questions for review and keep moving.
- **Answer before a break.** Anything you have seen before a break is locked afterward.
- **Never leave a blank.** A wrong answer costs nothing.
- **Need more time for language?** If the exam isn't available in your preferred language, you can request an additional 30 minutes. Microsoft asks for extra time and other accommodations to be requested before you schedule.
- **Read for the constraint.** AB-900 questions often hinge on a detail such as least privilege, no extra license, or what must stay unchanged.
